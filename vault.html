Sovereignty
3 Percent Global Inc.

Sovereignty is not a word we use lightly. It is the foundation on which every decision at 3 Percent Global is made across every branch — music, capital, knowledge, and community. Every asset owned. Every agreement built on equity. Every structure built to serve the people inside it — not extract from them.

Ownership, Not Rental

Every artist, operator, and aligned partner within the 3PG ecosystem owns what they create. This is not a courtesy — it is a structural requirement. No external party holds rights over what is built here.

No Permission Required

3PG was built to eliminate the need to ask for permission — from labels, from institutions, from systems, from anyone. The infrastructure exists so that the aligned can operate at the highest level on their own terms.

The 3% Standard

Every partnership, every deal, every collaboration is evaluated against one question: does this serve the long-term sovereignty of everyone involved? If the answer is no — we don't do it.

Chain of Title
3 Percent Global Inc.

3 Percent Global treats every idea produced inside its ecosystem as an asset — not content to give away. A song, a doctrine, a framework, a body of teaching: if it was built here, it gets registered, tracked, and administered like the asset it is.

What This Means

Every time a work carrying the 3PG name is used, played, cited, taught, or built upon — anywhere in the world — value returns to the ecosystem that produced it. Not to intermediaries. Here.

Our Model

Creators and contributors keep what they make. The holding administers — it does not own. Songwriter, teacher, writer, strategist: whatever the discipline, nobody signs their work away to 3 Percent Global. We collect and protect on your behalf. You keep your work.

Every Branch, One Standard

Music. Knowledge. Doctrine. Capital frameworks. Whatever the medium, the same rule holds: registered, protected, and accounted for — in every market this infrastructure touches.
