Who We Are

3 Percent Global is not a record label. It is a Global Sovereignty Infrastructure — a sovereign ecosystem built for those who refused to wait for permission.

Founded in Montreal — 3PG was born from a simple and irreversible decision: to build something real, something lasting, on terms that belong to no one but the creators themselves.

The name comes from a simple and uncomfortable observation: in any given field, in any given generation, only 3% of people operate at the level required to build something that lasts. The other 97% are waiting — for the right moment, the right deal, the right sign. The 3% build regardless.

We use music as a lever, not a ceiling. The objective is total freedom — financial, mental, and spiritual. Every project, every partnership, every decision made within 3PG is aligned with this singular mission. Nothing is done by accident.

We are a system of sovereign vessels. Not employees. Not artists waiting to be discovered. Builders. Architects of a new paradigm. The 3% who understand that true power is built — not given, not borrowed, and not begged for.

“We do not seek validation from the systems we are building to replace.”

“We were never building a brand. Brands trend. Infrastructure outlasts.”

“What we build, we hold. What we hold, we govern. What we govern, we pass on.”

“The 97% wait for permission. The 3% build what permission was meant to protect.”

The Four Pillars
— 01

Matrix Mastery

Understanding the systems that govern money, culture, and power — and operating above them. The matrix was not built for us. We build our own. Contracts, capital, media, information — not obstacles but instruments of sovereignty.

— 02

Financial Sovereignty

Every asset owned outright. Every deal built on equity, not exploitation. Generational wealth is not a byproduct — it is the objective. Each branch capitalized and governed as its own sovereign entity, with every revenue stream structured for permanence.

— 03

Spiritual Alignment

We do not manufacture. We transmit. Every release carries an undeniable truth rooted in who we are and where we come from — Gonaïves, Moroni, Montreal. Spirit before strategy. Always.

— 04

Knowledge Transmission

The Headquarters does not hoard knowledge. What we learn, we pass forward. The tools of liberation are shared within the alliance — city to city, generation to generation. The elevation of one is the elevation of all.

The Doctrine
Article I — On Entry

Nothing here is offered. What is aligned is recognized, and what is recognized is invited in. There is no application that guarantees passage — only alignment that reveals itself over time.

Article II — On Ownership

No operator, no contributor, and no branch of this infrastructure works for equity they do not hold. What is built is owned by those who build it. This is not negotiable, and it does not change with scale.

Article III — On Discretion

Not everything built here is built to be seen. Some instruments of sovereignty are most effective operating outside of public record. Discretion is not secrecy for its own sake — it is protection for what is still being built.

Article IV — On Standard

Every branch — capital, knowledge, culture, spirit — answers to the same standard, regardless of which came first. Growth in one branch that weakens another is not growth. It is exposure.

Article V — On Succession

3PG is not built around one person. It is built to outlive its founders, its first operators, and its first century. What is built for one generation is a project. What is built for the next is infrastructure.

No Permission Required

The 3% Standard
